# Commission — the rate table, the run, the payment sheet, and the cleared stamp

> Finance SOP A.1 "Receive the **daily (9:00 AM)** and **monthly (1st day of the following
> month)** commission schedule/report generated by the system."
> A.2 "Verify each RSM/agent's commission against sales records ... using the WATU data dashboard."
> A.3 "**Cross-check that each phone being paid for is correctly linked to the agent who sold
> it**, before approving payment."
> A.4 "Compile the full commission payment sheet and forward it to the **Administration approval
> group** for sign-off. The payment sheet must include: agent name, sales quantity, commission
> amount, phone number, and the RSM the agent falls under."
> A.6 "Submit the Yas statement for reconciliation and **mark the payment as 'cleared' in the
> system immediately, to prevent duplicate payment**."
> A "Commission Audit Checklist: for audit purposes, the following checklist must be completed
> and filed against every commission payment cycle."

**The point is A.6.** The arithmetic could be done in a spreadsheet. What a spreadsheet cannot
do is refuse to pay the same period twice.

| nav | pane | who you would tick it on |
|---|---|---|
| `commission` | Kamisheni / Commission | Finance — builds the cycle, sets the rates, records the payment |
| `commappr` | Idhini ya kamisheni / Commission sign-off | the Administration approval group |

Two navs on purpose. SOP A.4 puts the sign-off in somebody else's hands, so nobody can build a
sheet **and** approve it unless the owner deliberately ticks both.

## Before anybody opens a pane

1. **Run `db/migrations/RUN-ME-2026-09-09-commission.sql`** in Supabase. Safe to run more than
   once; until it is run both panes say so and name the file.
2. **Tick the navs** on the roles above.
3. **Set the rates.** Nothing is priced until somebody does, and a phone with no rate is
   reported as unpriced rather than quietly paid at zero.

## The rates

By **role** and **model**. `ANY` is the wildcard in either column and the most specific row
wins, so the lookup order is: this role and this model, then this role for any model, then any
role for this model, then the catch-all. A single company-wide rate is one row of `ANY / ANY`.

Rates are a table, not a constant in code — a rate that needs a deploy to change is a rate
somebody keeps in a spreadsheet instead.

## The run

Two shapes, exactly as A.1 describes: a **daily** cycle keyed by a date, and a **monthly** one
keyed by a month. A cycle is unique per period and kind in the database, which is the first half
of the duplicate-payment guard.

**A run is a snapshot, not a view.** The lines are written down when the cycle is built, because
a sheet that silently re-computes itself cannot be audited: the figure signed off on Tuesday must
still be the figure on Friday after another deck upload lands. Rebuilding is allowed while the
cycle is a draft and refused the moment anybody has signed.

Two kinds of phone are **held back** rather than paid, and both are counted and explained on the
agent's line:

- **Disputed** (A.3). The shop's own book credits the sale to a different agent than the Watu
  book does. Names that overlap are the same person; a genuine disagreement is not paid this
  cycle.
- **Unpriced.** No rate covers that role and model. It is not paid at zero — it is reported, so
  Finance can go and price it and rebuild.

## The sheet

The five fields A.4 requires, in the order the SOP lists them: agent name, sales quantity,
commission amount, phone number, and the RSM the agent falls under. The RSM comes from the same
roll-up the targets pane uses.

## Sign-off, payment, and clearing

The approval group opens the sheet and either signs it off or sends it back with a reason. A
sheet sent back returns to draft so Finance can fix the rates and rebuild.

Recording the payment needs **all five checklist rows ticked and a payment reference**. The
checklist is a gate, not a decoration:

1. Unpaid commission list (Hoop system)
2. Verified against WATU system data
3. Checked for any paid advance on top-ups
4. Approved payment voucher on file
5. Compared against bank/Yas statement

Paying stamps `cleared_at` in the same write, and every later attempt is refused by name. The
update is guarded on `cleared_at` still being empty, so two people pressing Pay at the same
instant produce one payment and one clear message, not two payments.
